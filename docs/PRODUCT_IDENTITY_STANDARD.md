# UNIBUD Product Identity Standard

## Core principle
Present each product by its established name, purpose, capabilities, and service identity. Do not routinely label products, apps, or agents as AI. Mention underlying technology only when relevant to technical documentation, safety, consent, or transparency. Do not change established navigation as part of this product-copy standard.

## Established navigation and spaces
- Primary navigation order remains: Square → Connect → Quad → Chat.
- The Guild is the umbrella for exactly three spaces: The Quarter, Concierge, and Board.
- Square is the social feed for posts, stories, and everyday expression.
- Connect is for discovering and connecting with people.
- Quad is for communities and shared interests.
- Chat is for direct conversations and messaging.
- SouLync remains the separate relationship/dating experience with its own privacy boundaries.
- Wayward remains a distinct culture, challenges, and discovery space.

## Product descriptions
- **The Guild:** The umbrella for The Quarter, Concierge, and Board.
- **The Quarter:** One integrated financial and commercial experience for money management, lending discovery, marketplace shopping, and payments.
- **Concierge:** A conversational personal-assistance service for guidance, requests, planning, and service coordination. It should feel like a conversation, not a generic errand form. A request is not a booking or payment until the provider confirms it.
- **Board:** The coordination and management space, including Spark Calendar and related academic activities.
- **SouLync:** A dedicated experience for dating and meaningful connections. Keep its profile and privacy boundaries separate from academic and social profiles.
- **Wayward:** A distinct space for culture, challenges, discovery, and shared experiences.
- **The Till:** Payments, transactions, receipts, and financial activity, where used within The Quarter. Do not rename established navigation to The Till.

## Presentation standards
1. Use the product's proper name consistently in page headings, descriptions, empty states, and supporting documentation.
2. Describe what users can do, what the service provides, and what is currently available.
3. Give each space a visual identity appropriate to its purpose while retaining shared UNIBUD brand standards.
4. Do not make unconnected features appear operational. Empty states must be honest and useful.
5. Do not change the established primary navigation or introduce replacement names for existing navigation items.
